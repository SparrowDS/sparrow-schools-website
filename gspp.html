Simplify Your GSPP Reporting

If your district received Golden State Pathways Program funding, you know the data reporting requirements can be complex and time-consuming. We're here to help.

The Challenge

GSPP requires districts to gather data from multiple sources, compile it correctly, and submit it to the California Department of Education. For small districts with limited staff capacity, this can create significant stress and uncertainty.

Our Solution

We provide step-by-step guidance for gathering the necessary data files, then handle all the processing work for you. You focus on your students and programs—we'll make sure your data is accurate and submitted correctly.

Your Peace of Mind

With 10 years of experience working directly with California student data systems and CDE reporting requirements, we understand exactly what's needed and how to get it right the first time.

How It Works

1. Initial Consultation

We'll schedule a brief call or meeting to understand your district's specific situation and GSPP grant details.

2. Guided Data Collection

We provide clear, step-by-step instructions for gathering the required data files from your student information system and other sources.

3. Professional Processing

Once you upload your files to us, we handle all the technical work: cleaning, compiling, validating, and formatting your data according to CDE specifications.

4. Review & Submission

We provide you with a complete summary of your processed data for review. If desired, we can also handle the submission to CDE on your behalf.

What's Included

✓ Data Collection Guide

✓ Complete Processing

✓ Quality Assurance

Timeline

From initial contact to final submission, the typical GSPP data processing project takes 2-3 weeks. We work on your timeline and can accommodate rush requests when needed.
