The Data Is Important. The Problem Is What Happens Next.

It Arrives in a Spreadsheet

The TOMS data download is built for data analysts. Distance from Standard, student growth percentiles, claim-level performance, subgroup disaggregations—it takes real expertise to pull meaningful signal from all of it, and most small districts don't have a dedicated analyst to do it.

The Stakes Are High

Your CAASPP results feed directly into your California Dashboard accountability ratings, shape your LCAP priorities, and define the story your community tells about your schools. You need to understand your data—and communicate it clearly—before someone else tells the story for you.

Two Things Have to Happen

Real improvement requires both system-level analysis and student-level action. You need to understand where your district is and where it's headed—and you need to be able to see individual students who need attention. Both matter. Most analysis tools give you one or the other.

Built from 15+ years in California education data

Every table, chart, and graph in this package has been designed with a specific question in mind. The structure of the analysis—what to look at, in what order, with what context—comes from over 15 years of direct work with California student data: in the classroom, in the district office, and in collaborative data sessions with leadership teams. We've led data analysis at admin retreats and school site PLCs. We've coached teams through the work of turning assessment results into instructional priorities. The analysis framework is ours; we use technology to execute it well at scale.

Your data is handled with the seriousness it deserves. All file transfer, storage, and communication of student data is fully FERPA compliant. You upload to a protected folder; we process and return your deliverables through the same secure channel. We never have direct access to your systems or any student records beyond what you share.
