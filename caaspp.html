We're currently developing comprehensive CAASPP analytics services including detailed performance reports, interactive dashboards, and trend analysis. Detailed breakdowns of student performance by grade level, subgroup, claim, and target to identify strengths and areas for growth.

Trend Reporting

Multi-year analysis showing how your students' performance changes over time, helping you evaluate the effectiveness of your programs.

Gap Analysis

Clear identification of achievement gaps between student subgroups, with recommendations for targeted intervention.

Teacher & School Reports

Customized reports at the classroom, grade, and school level to support teachers and administrators in their decision-making.
